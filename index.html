Universal Law I

Conservation of Energy

"Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it can only transform from one form to another."
Total Energy (\(\Delta E\)) = 0 (Constant)

In simple English: Energy in our universe never disappears. When you burn fuel, turn on a light bulb, or ride a bicycle, you are not creating new energy—you are simply guiding energy into a new outfit.

☀️ How Energy Transforms in Nature
01

Potential & Kinetic Harmony

Water sitting high in a dam stores potential energy. As it drops through turbines, it shifts to motion (kinetic) and electricity.

02

The Zero Waste Law

No machine or living being can ever destroy an ounce of energy. It radiates as warmth, sound, or light.

The Spiritual Insight: Change is merely the costume that energy wears. The essence remains forever untouched and infinite.
Universal Law II

Conservation of Matter

"Matter can neither be created nor destroyed by chemical or physical means."
Mass of Reactants = Mass of Products

In simple English: Every single atom in your body, in every leaf of every tree, and in the rivers was formed billions of years ago. When a tree falls or wood burns, not a single atom disappears—they simply reconnect into air, ash, and soil.

🔄 The Eternal Recycling of Atoms
💧

Prehistoric Drops of Water

The water droplets you drink today are the very same molecules that rained upon ancient forests millions of years ago.

The Burning Candle Secret

When candle wax melts and vanishes into thin air, the total weight of carbon dioxide + water vapor formed equals the original wax and oxygen combined!
