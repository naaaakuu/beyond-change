# Beyond Change 🌿

A peaceful, mobile-optimized experience weaving universal science and philosophy together.

## Highlights

- **Aesthetic**: Serene botanical greenery theme with tranquil animations, ambient emerald glows, and floating leaves.
- **Typography**: Flowing cursive script title — *"beyond change"*.
- **Featured Film**: Framed presentation of `mahavira.mp4` with custom ornamental corners and mobile-friendly playback (`playsinline`).
- **Universal Laws Explained in Simple English**:
  - **Conservation of Energy**: Principles, solar-to-life transformation SVG diagrams, and spiritual insights.
  - **Conservation of Matter**: The eternal recycling of atoms, real-world examples (water cycle & combustion), and Mahāvīra's fundamental doctrine (*Uppāne vā, vigame vā, dhuve vā*).
- **Connected Instagram Experience**: Direct embedded player and one-tap launch to the featured Reel.
- **Mobile First**: Tailored touch layout, fluid typography, and fast performance.
